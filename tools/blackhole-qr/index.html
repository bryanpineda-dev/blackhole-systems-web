:: STATIC_OWNERSHIP

Static QR, real ownership.

A static QR stores the final content directly inside the code. If it points to a URL, it keeps working as long as that destination stays online, without depending on a paid redirect dashboard.

  • No redirect layer The final payload lives inside the QR itself.
  • No dashboard dependency No account or external control panel required.
  • Local generation Your content is generated in the browser.
